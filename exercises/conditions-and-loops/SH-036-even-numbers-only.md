# SH-036 — Even Numbers Only

**Description**

Print every even number from 2 through a supplied nonnegative upper bound.

**Requirements**

Learn modulo `%`, arithmetic tests, and loops; consider what happens when the bound is below 2.

**Tips**

The remainder operator `%` distinguishes even and odd integers: an even number has remainder zero when divided by two. Combine a loop over the requested range with a numeric condition, or advance in steps of two. Make sure the bounds are correct and that only even values are printed.


**How to approach it:** Identify the required input, the expected output, and the command or Bash feature that connects them. Try a small controlled example first, then check the result against the example and the description. Keep paths and expanded variables quoted where appropriate; do not assume example paths or timestamps are fixed.

**Example**

```text
7 → 2 4 6
```
